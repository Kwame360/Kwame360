# Hi, I'm Kwame Yeboah 👋

Brand Identity & Product Designer with 6+ years building visual systems for startups and growth-stage companies across Africa and beyond. I specialise in the work that happens before a word is spoken — identities, interfaces, and experiences that earn trust at first glance.

Currently studying BSc. Software Engineering at GCTU (2024–2027) and closing the gap between design and code.

---

## What I do

**Brand Identity** — Logo systems, brand guidelines, colour palettes, and typography frameworks built to scale.

**UI/UX Design** — Responsive interfaces, design systems, wireframes, and prototypes with a focus on clarity and conversion.

**Web Design** — Frontend work in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and Next.js. I design it. I can also build it.

**Pitch Decks** — Visual storytelling for founders targeting investors and enterprise partners.
